sd-trapphone is a keypad burner phone for FiveM that runs a street business. Players serve corners, take drop-off orders, haggle by text, meet plugs and pay off crews, all from an early-2000s candybar handset with a colour screen, a d-pad and a full keypad. It comes in ten colours, each its own item with its own model in the hand, and it texts [sd-phone](https://github.com/Samuels-Development/sd-phone) both ways.

> [!TIP]
> **Running [sd-phone](https://github.com/Samuels-Development/sd-phone)? The two text each other.**
> A burner's text lands in the smartphone's Messages under the burner's number, and a reply there comes back to the burner. Nothing to set up, and sd-phone is not required.

## Highlights

- **A real handset.** Messages, Contacts, Hustle, Turf, Status, a Worm game, ringtones and wallpapers, driven by a d-pad, two soft keys and a keypad. Mouse, keyboard and on-screen keys all work, and players can still walk and drive with it out.
- **Corner sales.** Start serving and customers text an offer while they walk over, or pull up in a car at a spot you mark. Accept, name your own price, or turn them down.
- **Pitching and drop-offs.** Walk up to anyone and offer, or take orders while driving and deliver to doors across the map.
- **A name on the street.** XP and levels per product are kept on the phone itself, so a stolen phone is a stolen business. Customers remember you, and good ones become named regulars.
- **People.** Plugs, a supplier, an informant, a launderer, a fixer and each crew's shot caller reach out as your name grows.
- **Turf.** Every area has money, a product it craves and often a crew. Sell on their block without paying and they come and ask. Kill enough of them and they riot.
- **Back rooms.** A money wash and a black market that open once you know the right person.
- **Bagging up.** Battery-powered scales break bricks and ounces into smaller bags.
- **Heat.** Snitches and witnesses bring the police through your dispatch resource.

## Compatibility

| Layer | Supported |
|---|---|
| Frameworks | QBCore, QBox, ESX (auto-detected) |
| Inventories | ox_inventory, qb-inventory, qs-inventory, tgiann-inventory and others through the bridge (auto-detected) |
| Target | ox_target, qb-target, qtarget |
| Dispatch | linden_outlawalert, cd_dispatch, fd_dispatch, ps-dispatch, qs-dispatch, core_dispatch, origen_police, codem-dispatch, tk_dispatch |
| Phone | [sd-phone](https://github.com/Samuels-Development/sd-phone), optional: two-way texts |
| Languages | English, Simplified Chinese (`Locale` in `configs/config.lua`). Customer texts and names are set in `configs/` |

Per-phone progress needs an inventory that can store item metadata. On one that cannot, progress is kept per character instead.

## Installation

Download **`sd-trapphone.zip`** from the [latest release](https://github.com/Samuels-Development/sd-trapphone/releases/latest) and unzip it into your resources folder: it is the resource with its handset page already built, ready to drop in.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Download the `sd-trapphone.zip` asset, not **Source code (zip)** or the green **Code** button. Those are the source, without the built handset: the phone would open to nothing until you [build it yourself](#building-the-handset).

It needs [ox_lib](https://github.com/CommunityOx/ox_lib) and [oxmysql](https://github.com/CommunityOx/oxmysql). Start them and your framework first:

```cfg
ensure ox_lib
ensure oxmysql
ensure sd-trapphone
```

The database tables create themselves. Then:

1. Add the items from [items.md](items.md) to your inventory: the ten phones, scales, batteries, baggies and the product sizes. The icons are in `images/`.
2. Go through `configs/`. Prices, XP, stock and locations are in `configs/server/`, which never reaches a client.

Admins get `/trapadmin` (ace `command.trapphone`) to hand out phones, set XP, force a contact to reach out, set crew standing and reset a phone.

## For developers

How the parts fit together, the page protocol and every payload shape are in [docs/design.md](docs/design.md).

### Building the handset

The repository is the source: the built page (`web/build`) is not committed, and each [release](https://github.com/Samuels-Development/sd-trapphone/releases) builds it into its zip. To run a clone, or to change the page, build it with Node.js (LTS):

```bash
cd web
npm ci
npm run dev         # the handset in a browser, with sample data
npm run build       # what the game serves (web/build)
npm run build:demo  # the website's demo (web/build-demo): the handset alone, with a pretended street
```

A source zip unpacks to `sd-trapphone-main`: rename the folder to `sd-trapphone`, the name its exports are called by.

### Rebuilding the props

`stream/` holds one model per colour (`sd_trapphone_<colour>`, about 4,700 triangles, texture embedded). They are built from the handset page itself, so the prop matches what is on screen:

1. Capture `#prop=<colour>` and `#propback=<colour>` from the page (see `tools/make_textures.py`).
2. `python tools/make_textures.py` composes the textures and the inventory icons.
3. `blender --python tools/build_prop.py -- report.json` builds and exports every colour. Needs Blender 5.x with the Sollumz extension.

### Tests

`lua tests/run.lua` runs the offline checks for pricing, levels, zones, selling, drop-offs and dispatch under stock Lua 5.4.
